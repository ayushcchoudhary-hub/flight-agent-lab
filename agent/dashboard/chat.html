What have we actually compared?

Original synthetic-data suite: Astra low 75/75, Luna low 74/75. After fixing an airport-name parsing issue, both passed 5/5 repeats of that follow-up case. Median model times in the original run: Astra 4.63s, Luna 4.19s. Real staging: Astra low passed 6/6 flows in one run. The 12-setting screening passed all 180 attempts. We stopped repeated validation at your request after at least three complete repetitions per finalist; all 151 completed attempts passed (Luna low 50/50, Astra low 50/50, Terra medium 51/51). Five repetitions were not completed. The expanded comparison covers all four models at low, medium and high effort; see its live status and repetitions. These are development checks, not production accuracy estimates.

What this playground does—and what it doesn’t

Recorded mode: a real model interprets your message, then the application uses a saved staging response for the exact search. No fresh availability check. The clock is frozen to the capture date so defaults are reproducible.

Live mode: public search sends search-only requests to the CommonSwyft staging endpoint without an account token. The repository explicitly permits this and a staging search was verified on 19 September. Account-linked mode remains a separate, temporary connection; it never silently downgrades to public search if authentication fails.

This is the actual agent reply in a plain text chat, not an official WhatsApp preview. One-way, one traveler; business and today through seven days ahead are defaults. No booking, checkout or production changes. Forty messages per server run, fifteen per conversation; only one message runs at a time. Do not paste passwords or tokens into this chat.
