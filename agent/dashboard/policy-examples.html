policy

If I close my account, is all my analytics information automatically deleted?

policy

No. During the friends-and-family pilot, analytics profiles are not automatically removed when you close your account. Analytics events and performance measurements are retained for one year, and masked replays for 30 days. Provider expiry may leave copies briefly afterward. You can contact support to request deletion, but it is reviewed manually and is not automatic or immediate. Privacy policy: https://staging.commonswyft.com/privacy
